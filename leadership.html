Background

Two decades inside Mumbai real estate.

Before founding Tat:vm, Raahul spent over twenty years working across Mumbai's real estate landscape — in execution, development and redevelopment, including housing society and SRA projects. That ground-level experience, of what actually gets a project delivered, now shapes how Tat:vm plans, designs and builds.

Tat:vm exists because he believes a home in Mumbai shouldn't be a compromise on light, air or space just because the city is dense. Every project starts with how people will actually live in it — not with how much can be sold.
